I’m Lia Che, a student and researcher using scientific thinking to work through real-world problems. This site is a working archive of projects that bring together fieldwork, science, data, community work, design, and visual storytelling. Through interviews, field notes, photographs, experiments, and data, I build digital archives, illustrated projects, and research tools. I’m interested in how these different methods can come together to develop sustainable solutions.
