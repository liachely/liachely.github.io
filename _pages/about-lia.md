I’m Lia Che. My work moves between fieldwork, science, writing, data, photography, and visual storytelling. I am interested in how knowledge is gathered, how uncertainty is kept visible, and how lived experience can become an experience another person can enter without being flattened into a summary.

My current projects include **Atlas of Care**, a digital ethnography developed through travel, interviews, photographs, audio, location records, and field notes, and **Taming Your Elephant**, an illustrated interpretation of a Buddhist image painted by my grandmother.

This site is deliberately selective. It will grow as individual bodies of work are ready to be seen.
